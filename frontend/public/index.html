Library

Search books from Open Library, then borrow, renew, return and reserve them.

  • You can have up to 5 books on loan at a time.
  • A loan lasts 14 days and can be renewed 2 times.
  • Late fee: free for 3 days, then 5 kr per day, at most 200 kr.
  • If you owe 100 kr or more you cannot borrow.
